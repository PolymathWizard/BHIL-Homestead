Welcome to our Web site!

Nimbus Ledger Software, Inc. makes bookkeeping software for freelancers and small businesses. Nimbus Ledger Professional keeps your invoices, receipts, and tax reports in one place, so you can spend less time on paperwork and more time on your business. Click here to take the Product Tour.

Product Tour
Step 1: Send invoices. Enter a client once. Nimbus Ledger prints or e-mails the invoice every month and reminds you when payment is late.
Step 2: File receipts. Scan or type your receipts. Nimbus Ledger files each one by category and matches it to your bank statement.
Step 3: Print your tax report. One click prepares quarterly estimates and a year-end report your accountant can use.

Support

1. Do I need an accountant?
2. Can I import my spreadsheets?
3. Is my data secure?

1. Do I need an accountant? No, but Nimbus Ledger prints reports your accountant can use.
2. Can I import my spreadsheets? Yes. Save your spreadsheet as a .CSV file and choose File, Import.
3. Is my data secure? Your books are password protected and stored on your own computer. We recommend a weekly backup.
