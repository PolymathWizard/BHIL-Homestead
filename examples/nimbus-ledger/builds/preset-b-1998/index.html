What is Nimbus Ledger?

Hi! I'm Dana, and I wrote Nimbus Ledger because I was tired of doing my freelance books in a shoebox. Nimbus Ledger is a little program for Windows 95 that keeps track of your invoices and receipts and makes your tax report at the end of the year. It's not fancy, but it WORKS!

Invoices that remember themselves. Set up a client once and Nimbus prints the invoice every month. It even reminds you when they're late!
Receipts in one place. Type in your receipts or scan them and Nimbus files them by category.
Tax time in one click. Prints a year-end report your accountant can read. No more shoebox!

FAQ

Q: Do I need an accountant?
A: Nope! But Nimbus prints reports your accountant will like.

Q: Can I bring in my old spreadsheet?
A: Yes! Save it as a .CSV file and use File, Import.

Q: Is my data safe?
A: Your books stay on YOUR computer. Back up to a floppy every Friday!
