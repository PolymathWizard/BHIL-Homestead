# Code of conduct

HOMESTEAD is a playful project with serious rules about safety and rights. Contributors are expected to:

- Be kind and specific in reviews. Critique the build, not the builder.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Keep examples fictional or owned. Do not post other people's private sites, personal data, or copyrighted material in issues or pull requests.
- Never submit content that mocks real private individuals, or that sexualizes, harasses, or demeans anyone. Retro parody targets eras and brands, not people.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report problems through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
